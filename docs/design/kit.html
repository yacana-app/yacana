Sky · map with the mountWith the mount connected, the blue box is where the telescope points, live, and the yellow one is what you are going to frame; "Telescope", next to the photos' label, says which colour it is. "Go and centre" takes the telescope to the yellow one and corrects it with plate solving; "Go only" does just the GOTO. Tapping an object centres the map on it. Without a mount, the map is as it is now
