ASTAP's star databases are made from data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia (https://www.cosmos.esa.int/gaia), processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC, https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dpac/consortium). Funding for the DPAC has been provided by national institutions, in particular the institutions participating in the Gaia Multilateral Agreement.

Gaia data are distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0 IGO licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/igo/

Yacana does not carry the databases: they are downloaded from ASTAP's site or imported.
